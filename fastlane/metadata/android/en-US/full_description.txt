Track your trips with public transport - now on F-Droid too! You need a Träwelling account.

With this app, you can (among other things):
- Track your trips with public transport in Germany and other (european) countries
- Add an event to your trips
- Automatically share your trips on the fediverse
- Use your fediverse emojis
- See trips from friends and travel with them
- Set arrival/departure time
- Look at the route of the trip
- Easy check-in flow when wearing gloves through control with volume buttons

This app is a hobby project and in no association with Träwelling. This is not an official app by Träwelling.
Träwelcross is inspired by Träwelldroid.
